# Enumerator manual: Spec test survey

## 1. Purpose and study objectives
The study asks about children's health and schooling.

## 2. Roles and responsibilities
| Who | What |
|---|---|
| Enumerator | Interviews and uploads |

## 3. Survey protocols
Introduce the survey and read the consent text from the approved form. Select the child named on the tracking sheet. If the respondent refuses, thank them and record the reason. Replace a child only with approval. Make a call-back visit if the respondent is absent. Upload every interview each day and keep a backup. Keep everything respondents say confidential.

## 4. Using the tablet
Log in, work offline, and sync each day.

## 5. Description of the questions
### Module A Introduction
A1 enumerator_id: choose your name. A2 consent: ask whether the respondent agrees.
### Module B Household
B1 hh_age, B2 hh_sex, B3 hh_school (ask only if age is 6 or more), B4 hh_grade, B5 hh_meals.
### Module C Health
C1 hl_ill, C2 hl_clinic, C3 hl_cost, C4 hl_vaccine.

## 6. Key terms
School meal: a meal served at school.

## 7. Frequently asked questions
What if the child is not at school? Call your supervisor.
