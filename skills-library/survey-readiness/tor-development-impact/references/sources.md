## Reference structure of a complete TOR

Background (who commissions, the study, the data collection, where, when, respondents, interview lengths, mode). Scope of work (what the team provides, what the firm provides). Duties by activity. Field procedure plan contents. Training requirements. Deliverables and timeline. Data deliverables and documentation. Data quality. Qualifications. Confidentiality, ownership, re-use. Ethical provisions with an annex. Budget, contract, payment, remedies. Selection criteria with weights adding to 100. Downstream work.

## Known issues in the source TOR, corrected in the template

Activity numbers differed between the duties and the deliverables table. Later-phase activities had no deliverables, dates, or payment milestone. The background named three districts while later activities covered a fourth. "Survey Company" and "Survey Firm" were both used. Three different quality percentages appeared in three sections. The template fixes the numbering and the terms and flags the rest.
