# Reference structure for a survey firm TOR

Reference structure drawn from a standard survey firm TOR. **Project-specific
details (country, sample, instruments, dates, names, amounts) do not apply to other
projects and must never be raised as findings.** Use this only to check whether a
TOR covers the same kinds of content. The DECDI background, data confidentiality,
and ethical provisions text is standard across projects and should be present in
substance.

| Section | What a complete TOR contains |
|---|---|
| Background | Who commissions the work, the impact evaluation or study it serves, what data collection is needed, where, when, with which respondents and interview lengths, and the electronic mode |
| Scope of work | What the research team provides (sample list with contact and location details, questionnaires, programmed instrument, secure server, tracking lists for panel surveys, data quality checks run by the team) and what the firm provides (tablets, chargers, data plans, set-up) |
| Duties by activity | Permits and clearances; translation and integration into the form; pilot of the translated instrument; field procedure plan; recruitment, training, and evaluation of field staff; data collection with monitoring system, daily upload, backup, weekly reports, corrections, and final field report; any later phase |
| Field procedure plan contents | Adherence to the sample frame, revisits and substitution rules; panel tracking and attrition reasons (if panel); logistics; progress-tracking tools; backup and submission schedule; supervision and spot-check plan with coverage |
| Training requirements | Theory, classroom practice, field practice outside the sample, evaluation against practice scenarios; recruit more trainees than needed; a reserve of enumerators; selection based on evaluation |
| Deliverables and timeline | Table of outputs with dates, numbered consistently with the activities |
| Data deliverables | Raw and cleaned microdata delivered regularly during fieldwork; documentation (questionnaires in all languages with pilot changes, interviewer manual, field and quality procedures, ID and linkage guidance, codes for non-pre-coded items, problems and fixes, file and variable descriptions) |
| Data quality | Share and method of spot checks and back-checks, accompaniment, and the team's own checks |
| Qualifications | Legal status; prior experience with the population and context; logistics capacity; staff network and named managers; impact evaluation background; references; electronic data collection and the survey software; data management; hardware; a quality-control system open to the research team; panel experience if relevant |
| Confidentiality, ownership, re-use | Data are confidential and owned by the commissioning institution; compliance with the data security standards; no use or distribution to third parties without written approval |
| Ethical provisions | Informed consent required; compliance with national and review board requirements; an annex with ethical principles (approval, avoid harm, voluntariness and withdrawal, confidentiality, local law and cultural sensitivity) |
| Budget, contract, payment | Contract period; payment milestones tied to approved deliverables and covering every activity; remedies for non-compliance with stated integrity thresholds (for example share of interviews not conducted or inconsistently completed); the research team's own verification checks |
| Selection criteria | Criteria, sub-criteria, and weights that sum to 100 and match the qualifications section |
| Downstream work | Whether the firm may be considered for later rounds |
