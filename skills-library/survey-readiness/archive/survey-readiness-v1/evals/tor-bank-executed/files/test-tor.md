# Terms of Reference: Household Survey Firm (TEST DOCUMENT, invented project)

Procurement: the firm will be procured by the World Bank. Estimated contract value USD 120,000. Single-source selection is proposed.

## 1. Background
The Bank's research team is running an impact evaluation of a farm advisory program in the fictional country of Zandia. A firm is needed to interview 2,400 households by tablet (SurveyCTO). Interviews last about 60 minutes.

## 2. Scope of work
The firm will provide tablets and train enumerators. The research team will provide the sample list and the programmed form.

## 3. Duties
3.1 Translate the questionnaire into two languages.
3.2 Pilot the translated questionnaire.
3.3 Train enumerators.
3.4 Collect data and upload it daily.
3.5 Submit a final field report.

## 4. Training
Enumerators will be trained for three days in a classroom.

## 5. Deliverables and payment
| No. | Deliverable | Due | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Translated questionnaire | 15 March | 20% |
| D2 | Pilot report | 10 March | 20% |
| D3 | Training completed | 1 April | 20% |
| D4 | Final dataset | 30 June | 40% |

Work starts on 1 March. Fieldwork runs from 15 April to 15 June.

## 6. Data quality
The firm will back-check 5% of interviews. Supervisors will spot-check 15% of interviews in the field. Back-checks cover 10% of interviews in the first two weeks.

## 7. Selection criteria
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Experience with household surveys in the country | 30 |
| Staff qualifications | 25 |
| Electronic data collection capacity | 20 |
| Price | 15 |
