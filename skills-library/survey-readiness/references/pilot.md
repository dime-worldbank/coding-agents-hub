# Pilot plan review

**Basis:** DIME Wiki *Survey Pilot*; Impact Analytics survey review resources.
**Documents:** pilot plan, respondent selection protocol, protocols for any extra data, the plan for recording changes.
**Rule:** judge whether the pilot will test what the team needs to learn. Do not set sample sizes. The usual three stages are a suggestion: pre-pilot (design and context), content test (paper), and data test (programmed form). Some can run at the same time, and an adapted or follow-up instrument may skip early ones.

## Contents the pilot should test

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PILOT-001 | The plan lists what will be tested: question comprehension and wording; order and flow; completeness of answer choices; sensitive questions; interview time on the shortest and longest paths; translations; the programmed form (skips, repeats, order); missing fields in the data; data quality checks and back-check templates; protocols and logistics | Important | Add missing items |
| PILOT-002 | Pilot respondents are outside the main sample | Blocker | Draw pilot units outside the sample |
| PILOT-003 | Pilot respondents resemble the target population; selection and replacement protocol is written | Important | Document selection |
| PILOT-004 | The final programmed form is tested in all languages on the real devices | Important | Pilot the deployed version |
| PILOT-005 | Pilot data are loaded and the planned data quality checks and back-check templates are run on them | Important | Add to plan |
| PILOT-006 | A plan exists for recording changes and updates (change log). If none is given, suggest one: date, finding, source, question or variable ID, change made, form version, who approved | Important | Ask for the plan or adopt the suggested log |
| PILOT-007 | Pilot findings feed the enumerator manual and the final instrument | Important | Add a feedback step |
| PILOT-008 | Time and budget exist to revise and re-test after the pilot (see TIME-002) | Important | Add revision time |
| PILOT-009 | Ethics approval and consent cover the pilot (see ETH-001) | Blocker | Obtain approval |

## Suggested

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PILOT-010 | Content tests are done on paper and before programming; data tests on the programmed form | Minor | Sequence or combine stages deliberately |
| PILOT-011 | Interviewers probe more than usual, respondents think aloud, and notes on timing, confusion, and discomfort are recorded | Minor | Add an observation guide |
| PILOT-012 | Team members observe interviews, including those who do not speak the language | Minor | Plan observation |
| PILOT-013 | Pilot is separate from enumerator field practice | Minor | Separate them (see TIME-012) |
| PILOT-014 | Protocols for any extra data (plot outlines, child measurements) | Minor | Write them |
| PILOT-015 | A local mobilizer or equivalent to coordinate respondents, where useful | Minor | Consider |

**Ask if missing:** whether the instrument is new, adapted, or a follow-up; planned pilot sample and sites; how changes will be recorded.
