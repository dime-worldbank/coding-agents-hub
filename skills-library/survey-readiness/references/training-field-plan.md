# Enumerator training and field plan review

**Basis:** Impact Analytics survey review resources (training and field plan requirements); DIME Wiki *Survey Pilot* and *Data Quality Assurance Plan*.
**Documents:** training manual and schedule, enumerator and supervisor manuals, field plan, back-check protocol.
**Rule:** check coverage and consistency with the other documents. Judge nothing about local logistics the documents do not describe.

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRAIN-001 | Training has theory, classroom practice, field practice outside the sample, and an evaluation | Important | Add missing components |
| TRAIN-002 | More people trained than hired, with a reserve; selection depends on evaluation results | Important | Add |
| TRAIN-003 | Training length and schedule fit the instrument; the pilot finishes before training | Important | Adjust |
| TRAIN-004 | Enumerator and supervisor manuals reflect pilot findings and the final instrument | Important | Update |
| TRAIN-005 | Roles are defined: enumerators, team leaders, supervisors, data managers | Minor | Define |
| TRAIN-006 | Field plan states adherence to the sample, revisit and substitution rules, respondent selection, and tracking rules | Important | Add |
| TRAIN-007 | Daily progress tracking sheets and an information system for interviews completed, units not found, and replacements | Important | Add |
| TRAIN-008 | Data upload, backup, and weekly reporting schedule; what to do if a device fails | Important | Add |
| TRAIN-009 | Back-check protocol with coverage, timing, and who conducts it, matching the DQA plan | Important | Reconcile |
| TRAIN-010 | Logistics, safety, and contact protocol for enumerators and respondents | Minor | Add |
| TRAIN-011 | Plan for correcting problems found by quality checks, including revisits | Important | Add |
| TRAIN-012 | Field plan, TOR, and timeline agree on dates and responsibilities | Important | Reconcile |
| TRAIN-013 | Devices meet SurveyCTO's current requirements: Android 4.4 or higher (iOS 11 or higher) at minimum; Android 5 or higher recommended, since some 4.4 devices struggle with photos and barcodes; Android 6 or higher for local Wi-Fi sync; at least 4 GB storage (8 GB recommended) and 2 GB memory (4 GB recommended, especially for long forms); a dedicated GPS chip if GPS is collected; a SIM card where Wi-Fi is limited. Devices should run the newest Android they support | Important | Specify devices and check them against SurveyCTO's current support pages. The specifications here may be out of date; verify them with a web search when one is available |
| TRAIN-014 | Spare devices, chargers, and power banks planned for loss, damage, and low power | Minor | Add spares |

**Ask if missing:** whether the firm or the team writes the field plan; planned training length.
