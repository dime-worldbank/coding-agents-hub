# Hiring a survey firm review (TOR and procurement)

**Basis:** the reference TOR structure (`tor-reference.md`); Impact Analytics survey review resources; DECDI guide to remote data collection (contract changes).
**Documents:** draft TOR, selection criteria, single-source justification if any.
**Rule:** check the TOR for completeness and internal consistency. Project-specific details are not findings. This module does not check compliance with procurement rules.

**First establish who executes the procurement:**
- **Bank-executed:** the firm is procured by the World Bank. Apply the common checks and the Bank provisions below.
- **Recipient-executed:** the firm is procured by a government, ministry, or statistics office. Apply the common checks, then the recipient-executed checks. Procurement follows the recipient's rules.

## Common checks

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROC-001 | TOR covers each section in `tor-reference.md` | Important | Add missing sections |
| PROC-002 | Scope states what the team provides and what the firm provides (sample list, instruments, programmed form, server, devices, tracking lists) | Important | Add |
| PROC-003 | Activities, deliverables table, and payment schedule are numbered consistently and refer to the same things | Important | Renumber and reconcile |
| PROC-004 | Every activity (including any later phase) has deliverables, dates, and a payment milestone | Important | Add missing ones |
| PROC-005 | Deliverable dates are consistent with the stated start of work and with each other | Important | Reconcile |
| PROC-006 | Field procedure plan content is specified: revisits, substitutions, tracking, logistics, backup, supervision | Important | Add |
| PROC-007 | Training requirements: theory, classroom practice, field practice outside the sample, evaluation, extra trainees and a reserve | Important | Add |
| PROC-008 | Quality provisions: spot-check and back-check coverage, daily upload, regular reporting, corrections, the team's own checks; consistent with the DQA plan | Important | Reconcile with DQA plan |
| PROC-009 | Data deliverables and documentation (raw data regularly, IDs and linkage, codes, file and variable descriptions) | Important | Add |
| PROC-010 | Payment tied to approved deliverables; remedies for integrity failures with thresholds | Important | Add |
| PROC-011 | Selection criteria weights sum to 100 and match the qualifications section | Important | Fix weights |
| PROC-012 | TOR requires devices that meet SurveyCTO's current requirements (see TRAIN-013), with chargers, data plans, set-up, and a replacement plan | Important | Add device specification |
| PROC-013 | TOR matches the sampling protocol, timeline, pilot plan, and DQA plan | Important | Reconcile |
| PROC-014 | Quality coverage figures do not conflict inside the TOR | Minor | Make consistent |
| PROC-015 | Confidentiality, ownership, and re-use text; data security standards; encryption expectations stated | Blocker | Add |
| PROC-016 | Ethical provisions: consent, review board compliance, and the ethical principles annex | Blocker | Add |

## Single-source selection

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROC-017 | Single source for contracts below USD 50,000 is allowed. Above USD 50,000 it is possible only with a written justification giving task- and firm-specific reasons and the alternatives considered | Important | Add or strengthen the justification. Threshold as provided by the review owner; not verified against a document |

## Bank-executed: Bank provisions

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROC-018 | World Bank privacy office approval is in place for the data collection. Point the team to the privacy office and ask for the status | Blocker | Obtain approval before collection. Rule as provided by the review owner; confirm the process |
| PROC-019 | Bank provisions are in the TOR: data and intellectual property owned by the Bank, confidentiality, compliance with the data security standards, no third-party use without written approval, ethical provisions annex, right to repeat work or withhold payment on integrity failures, and the Bank's own verification checks | Important | Add |
| PROC-020 | Changes to scope, mode, timeline, or budget after contract signing follow a contract modification, which for Bank funding needs Corporate Procurement approval | Minor | Note in the plan |

## Recipient-executed

| ID | What to check | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROC-021 | The TOR gives the research team written access to the data, the instrument, quality-check outputs, and field reports | Important | Add |
| PROC-022 | Equivalent confidentiality, data ownership, ethics, and security provisions are in the recipient's contract | Important | Add |
| PROC-023 | The recipient's procurement rules and approvals are identified and the timeline allows for them | Important | Confirm with the recipient |

**Ask if missing:** who procures the firm; contract value (for the single-source rule); who programs the questionnaire; whether the pilot happens before or after hiring.
