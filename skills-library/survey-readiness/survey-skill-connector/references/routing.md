# Routing and what no skill covers

## Which skill for which request

| The team wants | Skill | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Review a programmed form, compare it with the paper questionnaire, make a form from paper, make paper from a form | `xlsform-paper-sync` | Universal |
| A prep plan with every step, or a timeline from the start date or from what is done | `survey-prep-timeline` | Universal |
| A TOR to hire a survey firm, or a TOR review | `tor-development-impact` | Development Impact |
| An enumerator manual or field manual | `enum-manual` | Universal |
| A training agenda, quizzes, final test, field practice, selection | `enum-training-plan` | Universal |
| A data quality plan, high-frequency checks, back-checks | `hfc-plan`: planned, not available yet. Say so. Point to the iehfc package and the Development Impact Wiki High Frequency Checks page | Universal |

Check the available skills list. If the routed skill is not installed, say so in one line, point to its README, and stop. Do not do its work from memory.

## Suggested order of preparation

1. Objectives, indicators, sampling plan. 2. Ethics approval and consent. 3. Questionnaire design and content test. 4. Pilot. 5. Programmed form, and the paper-versus-form comparison. 6. TOR and hiring the firm. 7. Enumerator manual. 8. Training plan. 9. Data quality plan. 10. Field plan. The timeline runs through all of it.

## No skill yet: pointers only

These are not reviews. Give the pointer, name the owner, and put the step in the timeline.

| Area | Pointer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Research ethics and data security | Development Impact Wiki: Research Ethics and Data Security. Development Impact consent templates (written, oral, SMS) and data security standards, linked in the TOR template | Wiki pages dated 2023 name specific tools. Confirm current approved tools. Approval must cover the pilot. If the World Bank procures data collection, privacy office approval is needed (rule supplied by the review owner, not verified) |
| Sampling | Development Impact Wiki: Sampling | Needs a frame or listing, a second person checking the sampling code, and unique IDs used in the form. Consult Impact Analytics before the sample goes to the field |
| Pilot plan | Development Impact Wiki: Survey Pilot | Pilot respondents outside the main sample. Test comprehension, flow, timing, translations, the programmed form, and protocols. Keep a change log. Field practice is not the pilot |
| Questionnaire design | Development Impact Wiki: Questionnaire Design (most recent source, 2025) | Not reviewed by any skill |
| Data quality plan | iehfc package and its introduction on the World Bank blog | `hfc-plan` is on hold |

Wiki pages are on dimewiki.worldbank.org. Search for the page title if a link has moved.
